# Contributing to MangaWeave

Thanks for contributing to MangaWeave.

MangaWeave is developed in public under `AGPL-3.0-only`. The project also preserves the option to offer separate commercial licenses, so contributions are accepted under the Contributor License Agreement in [CLA.md](CLA.md).

## Before submitting a contribution

Please make sure that:

- you have the right to submit the code, documentation, tests, examples, or other material you are contributing;
- your contribution does not copy third-party material in a way that conflicts with its license or copyright;
- generated or model-assisted material has been reviewed by you and can be submitted under the Project's licensing terms; and
- you have read and agree to [CLA.md](CLA.md).

By submitting a pull request or other contribution for inclusion in MangaWeave, you indicate agreement to the CLA unless the Project maintainers have approved another written arrangement.

## Licensing of accepted contributions

Accepted contributions are distributed as part of MangaWeave under `AGPL-3.0-only`, unless a file clearly states otherwise.

The CLA also grants the Project the rights needed to offer separate commercial or proprietary licenses. Contributors retain ownership of their contributions.

## Project direction

MangaWeave focuses on manga-native sequential storytelling: narrative beats, page rhythm, panel hierarchy, character acting, reading flow, dialogue topology, black-and-white print language, and generation/editing guidance that treats a manga page as a coupled system rather than an illustration grid.

Changes should preserve that direction rather than optimize only for a single example, model, benchmark, or visual style.
