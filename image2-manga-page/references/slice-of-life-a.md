# Slice-of-Life A

## Identity

Reaction-driven, steady-panel everyday manga.

Structural study reference: works such as *Yotsuba&!*.

The goal is not to imitate a specific artist. Extract the narrative logic.

## Narrative engine

**small event → reaction → reaction develops or bends → tiny payoff**

The event can be trivial.

The behavior must be specific.

## What carries the page

Primary:
- facial expression
- body language
- timing
- dialogue rhythm
- repeated framing
- practical action

Secondary:
- background
- atmosphere
- dramatic composition

## Panel tendency

Prefer:
- regular rectangular panels
- stable camera geography
- medium shots
- full-body action when posture matters
- face close-ups
- repeated or nearly repeated framing

Avoid:
- flashy page design
- excessive diagonals
- decorative border-breaking
- constant dramatic angle changes

Core rule:

> Let character behavior create the entertainment.

## Hierarchy tendency

Slice-of-life pages do not need aggressive visual distortion.

But they still need:
- one more important beat
- one quieter reaction or pause
- one landing

Do not let stability become sameness.

## Background strategy

The setting must be clear, but background density should be low after orientation is established.

Use:
- one or two useful environment panels
- simplified reaction backgrounds
- white space where appropriate
- only relevant objects

Do not reduce the whole page to floating heads.

## Character acting

Prefer:
- exaggerated but readable expressions
- clear silhouette and posture
- awkward gestures
- blunt or delayed reactions
- calm/chaotic contrast
- small physical business

Characters should look caught in life, not posed for a picture.

## Dialogue rhythm

Speech bubbles often act as a timing grid.

Useful rhythms:
- statement
- response
- pause
- delayed response
- repeated word
- flat answer after absurd logic

Silence can be the punchline.

## Typical failure

### Too cinematic
Fix by flattening panel grammar.

### Too pretty
Fix by increasing behavioral specificity.

### Too empty
Restore one spatial anchor.

### Feels like illustration collage
Strengthen panel-to-panel causality and repeated setups.

### Too evenly sparse
Reintroduce one stronger beat, one smaller reaction panel, and a clearer landing.

## Success test

Ask:
- Is a small everyday event enough to carry the page?
- Are reactions more important than spectacle?
- Does the page feel observed rather than staged?
- Could some panels look plain and still be necessary?

If yes, the mode is working.
