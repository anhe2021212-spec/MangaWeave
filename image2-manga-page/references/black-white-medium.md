# True Black-and-White Manga Medium

## Hard requirement

The result must look like manga designed for black-and-white print.

It must **not** look like:
- grayscale illustration
- sepia illustration
- beige manga filter
- monochrome anime render
- soft digital painting with panel borders

## Material logic

Use:
- pure white paper
- black ink
- solid black fills
- visible screentone dots
- hatching
- cross-hatching
- kakeami / line-based tone
- controlled white cut-outs
- blank white areas

All middle values should visually read as print texture or line texture.

## Skin

Prefer:
- mostly untouched white paper
- restrained tone only where needed
- minimal modeling

Avoid:
- soft gray skin shading
- smooth digital volume
- airbrushed cheek shadows

## Hair

Prefer:
- grouped strand shapes
- clear silhouette
- black masses for dark hair
- white cut highlights where appropriate
- selective interior lines

Avoid:
- thousands of rendered hair strands
- glossy painted hair
- soft gray hair volume

## Clothing

Use clothing to create black/white structure.

Dark clothing may become a strong solid-black mass.

Light clothing should remain mostly white with line folds and selective tone.

## Background

Background does not need photographic realism.

Use:
- contour
- selective perspective
- tone fields
- black masses
- line texture
- omitted detail

A background can become sparse or nearly blank if the story beat no longer needs spatial detail.

## Strict failure signs

Treat the medium as failed when any of these dominate:

- sepia or beige cast
- warm gray overall wash
- continuous grayscale painting
- smooth skin rendering
- painterly hair
- cinematic soft light
- bokeh
- volumetric glow
- illustration-style depth grading
- screentone used only as a decorative overlay

Composition can be excellent and the result can still fail this skill if the medium is wrong.

## Positive production reminder

Do not only “remove color.”

Actively build the page from:
- white paper
- black masses
- line drawing
- tone patches
- open empty areas

The viewer should feel that the page was conceived for monochrome print from the beginning.
